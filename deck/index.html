The Vision

For a decade, women wait for an endometriosis diagnosis while biological evidence leaves their body every month. Feminnova reads it.

The Problem

Women with endometriosis wait an average of 7 to 10 years for diagnosis, some over 20. The current pathway offers no non-invasive early test: standard ultrasound frequently misses early-stage lesions, and confirmation requires laparoscopic surgery. The market demand exists. The tool does not.

The Solution

Menstrual blood contains cells shed directly from the endometrium. Endometriosis leaves two traceable marks in these cells: altered DNA methylation patterns and a shifted cell-type composition. Together, they form a molecular signature specific enough to distinguish disease from normal variation.

Dual-Signatures
Feminnova's pipeline computationally extracts epigenetic, immune, and inflammatory signatures from a single DNA methylation workflow.
Timeline Accelerated
Delivers a clear, actionable molecular risk score within 5 to 7 days from a non-invasively collected home sample.

The Technical Novelty

Existing approaches read systemic signals from peripheral blood, missing localized biology. Feminnova reads the shedding tissue directly. While single-modality benchmarks exist, our novelty is a population-specific multi-omic classifier. This approach not only provides accuracy and insights, but also takes the diagnostic pathway from years to days.

Proof of Concept Validated
A 2025 proof-of-concept study confirmed this approach, distinguishing endometriosis with over 80% accuracy without compounding assay complexity or cost.
