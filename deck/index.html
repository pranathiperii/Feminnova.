The Macro Problem

Endometriosis is a global invisible epidemic, acting as a chronic inflammatory condition where tissue mimicking the uterine lining establishes outside the uterus. Because historical evaluation methods depend on subjective tracking and high-cost surgical confirmations, millions face systemic diagnostic gaps.

Global Diagnostic Gaps

Current validation approaches miss structural anomalies entirely during early, superficial stages. Across major health networks, wait times for specialized gynecology evaluations range from 6 to 18 months, leading to prolonged symptom management without concrete answers.

The Target Market Focus

While building for global scale, initial test deployments target India's highly fragmented healthcare ecosystem, where 42 million women are affected[cite: 1]. The Feminnova Pipeline

Moving molecular diagnostic evaluation out of specialized infrastructure and into a swift, non-invasive timeline[cite: 1].

Step 01
Sample Collection
Day 2 menstrual collection at home using a specialized, non-invasive collection cup or swab[cite: 1]. Complete personal privacy with no clinical infrastructure required[cite: 1].
Step 02
Lab Processes
Menstrual Stem Cell (MenSC) isolation combined with Reduced Representation Bisulfite Sequencing (RRBS) profiling on Next-Gen Sequencing platforms[cite: 1].
Step 03
Risk Report
Proprietary machine learning classifiers process multi-omic data to deliver a High, Moderate, or Low risk score with calculated confidence intervals within 5 to 7 days[cite: 1].

The Deep-Science Frontier

Bypassing systemic inflammation signals by evaluating the localized tissue of origin directly[cite: 1].

Why Menstrual Tissue?

  • Direct access to exfoliating endometrial cells of origin[cite: 1]
  • Reflects localized pelvic disease biology, bypassing vague peripheral blood signals[cite: 1]
  • Yields millions of viable, highly informative live cells per sample[cite: 1]

The Epigenetic Moat

  • Consistently maps HOXA10 and HOXA11 hypermethylation variants across independent cohorts[cite: 1]
  • Captures ESR1 hypermethylation signatures that drive progesterone resistance[cite: 1]
  • Leverages 51 distinct methylation quantitative trait loci (mQTLs) linked directly to disease GWAS data[cite: 1].

Clinical Proof of Concept

Validated on computational and cohort benchmarks (n=42), our integrated machine learning classifier achieved 83% specificity and 79% sensitivity using targeted MenSC methylation signatures[cite: 1].
