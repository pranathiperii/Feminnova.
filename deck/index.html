The Vision

For a decade, women wait for an endometriosis diagnosis while biological evidence leaves their body every month. Feminnova reads it.

The Macro Problem

Endometriosis is a chronic inflammatory disease where tissue similar to the uterine lining grows outside the uterus, causing severe pain and structural complications. Because existing diagnostic pathways rely heavily on subjective symptom tracking and invasive surgeries, millions remain unmanaged.

The Friction in the Current Pathway

The standard path moves from delayed symptom tracking to inconclusive pelvic imaging, followed by lengthy specialist wait times. A definitive confirmation currently requires a laparoscopic surgery costing between INR 50k to 2L. Because of cost, accessibility barriers, and diagnostic stigma, many individuals never reach surgical confirmation.

The Feminnova Pipeline

Moving molecular diagnostic evaluation out of specialized infrastructure and into a swift, non-invasive timeline.

Step 01
Sample Collection
Day 2 menstrual collection at home using a specialized, non-invasive collection cup or swab. No clinical infrastructure required.
Step 02
Lab Processes
Menstrual Stem Cell (MenSC) isolation combined with Reduced Representation Bisulfite Sequencing (RRBS) profiling on Next-Gen Sequencing platforms.
Step 03
Risk Report
Proprietary machine learning classifiers process multi-omic data to deliver a High, Moderate, or Low risk score within 5 to 7 days.

The Deep-Science Frontier

Bypassing systemic inflammation signals by evaluating the localized tissue of origin directly.

Why Menstrual Tissue?

  • Direct access to exfoliating endometrial cells of origin
  • Reflects localized pelvic disease biology, bypassing vague peripheral blood signals
  • Yields millions of viable, highly informative live cells per sample

The Epigenetic Moat

  • Consistently maps HOXA10 and HOXA11 hypermethylation variants across cohorts
  • Captures ESR2 hypermethylation drives progesterone resistance
  • Leverages 51 distinct methylation quantitative trait loci (mQTLs) linked to structural disease

Clinical Proof of Concept

Validated on computational and cohort benchmarks (n=42), our integrated machine learning classifier achieved 83% specificity and 79% sensitivity using targeted MenSC methylation signatures.
